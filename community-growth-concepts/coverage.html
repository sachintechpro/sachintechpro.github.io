    Estate & family
    Why discuss a will or trust?

    Families may want to plan for beneficiaries, decision-makers and guardianship. A qualified California estate-planning attorney can assess suitable legal documents.

    What if an aging parent needs help?

    Start with your parent's wishes, authorized decision-makers and questions about care options, benefits and costs.

    Protection & savings
    What should I ask about indexed universal life?

    Ask a licensed and authorized professional about insurance costs, policy charges, credited interest assumptions, risks of lapse and alternatives.

    How do we plan for a child's education?

    Clarify your goals and timeframe, then ask a qualified professional about education savings choices, potential tax treatment, fees and restrictions.
