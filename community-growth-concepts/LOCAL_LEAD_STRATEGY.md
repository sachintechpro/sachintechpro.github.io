# Hello, Nisha — Northridge family connections (demo)

## Purpose
This is a local relationship and educational lead-generation concept for people within approximately 25 miles of Northridge, California. It is NOT a Farmers Insurance marketing site, Farmers product catalog, legal practice, or investment advisory service.

## Visitor pathways
1. Estate planning, wills and trusts → questions for a California-licensed estate-planning attorney.
2. Family protection and indexed universal life (IUL) → discuss mechanics, costs, risks, and licensed/appointed insurance professionals.
3. Savings and long-term financial goals → consultation with a qualified, appropriately licensed financial professional where needed.
4. Coverage gaps → evaluate existing benefits, exclusions and possible supplemental needs with a qualified licensed professional.
5. Aging parents → care coordination, healthcare directives, cost questions, and specialist referrals.
6. Children's education → education savings questions and future planning.

Secondary areas: community connections, workshops, and qualified professional partnerships. Home, auto and umbrella are not the central focus; do not automatically cross-sell any product until Nisha's agency/carrier authorizations and employer agreement permit it.

## Lead generation experience
Every pathway ends in **Connect with Nisha** with a preselected interest topic. Contact forms and newsletter requests currently simulate success locally only. No lead is saved, no email/SMS is transmitted, and a visitor cannot book an actual meeting. Future production requires secure consent-based lead storage, verified provider integrations, approved newsletter double opt-in/unsubscribe, real scheduling, and clear service/provider disclosures.

## Review before going live
- Have Nisha verify employer/agency contract restrictions, approved product lines, outside-business activities, branding, referrals and compensation, particularly any line that could compete with Farmers or a partner.
- Confirm her California license(s), appointments, and permission to market each insurance product before representing her as able to recommend or sell it.
- Estate documents and legal advice belong to licensed attorneys; financial/investment advice belongs to appropriately licensed professionals. Avoid implying Nisha supplies those services unless verified.
- Follow California insurance advertising license-number and other disclosure requirements where applicable.
- Obtain consent for contact, text, and newsletter messaging; separately document opt-in and unsubscription before real campaigns.
- Preserve the Northridge + ~25-mile service-area focus without falsely representing all towns as served or claiming actual partnerships.

Content should orient visitors and invite conversation rather than provide individualized recommendations or downloadable research. Demo-only claims must remain visible.
