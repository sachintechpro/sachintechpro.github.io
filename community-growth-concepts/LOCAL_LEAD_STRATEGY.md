# Hello, Nisha — local relationship concept

## Goal

Build professional relationships and potential **permitted** introductions among families roughly 25 miles from Northridge, California, independently of Farmers' core home/auto/umbrella marketing. This is a concept proposal, not an approved operating or commercial program.

## Six visitor pathways

- Estate planning, wills, trusts, guardianship → educational questions; legal documents/advice by qualified California attorneys.
- Family protection and indexed universal life → explain costs, limitations, lapses and trade-offs; licensed/appointed professionals for any products.
- Savings and longer-term goals → decision preparation and consultation with appropriately qualified financial professionals.
- Coverage gaps and supplemental protection → understand existing benefits, exclusions and cost exposure, subject to permissions.
- Aging parents → care choices, decision-makers, family discussions and appropriate specialist introductions.
- Children's education → education funding questions, timelines and planning considerations.

## Current funnel

Visitors choose a topic on Home, get a question guide, explore hypothetical community examples, then may use the digital guide, open a real device email/text/call handoff, or test a collapsible **demo-only** inquiry/newsletter preview.

The website **does not collect, store, sell or forward leads** through preview forms. It **does send chat questions to an AI provider** for guide responses. Email drafts and SMS drafts remain in the visitor's own device until they choose to send. Booking requires a real human confirmation.

## Business boundaries

Nisha must verify Farmers/agency restrictions on outside business, use of agency email/phone, referral fees, carrier appointments, licensing, website branding and marketing before actual outreach. Avoid claiming she can provide legal documents, investment advice, insurance recommendations or sell specific products unless verified. Obtain explicit consent and implement compliant privacy, storage, unsubscribe and communications controls before launching an actual lead system.
