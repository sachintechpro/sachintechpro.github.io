# Family-owned multi-branch service business market

## Ideal customer profile
- U.S.-based, privately held or family-controlled service/installation business.
- Approximately $5M+ annual revenue.
- 2–20 operating branches or meaningful service territories.
- Owner, president, COO or operations leader remains accessible.
- Operations still depend on spreadsheets, email, phone calls and disconnected systems.
- Multiple handoffs across sales, estimating, scheduling, suppliers, field crews, finance and warranty.

## Highest-priority verticals
1. Window and door replacement / installation
2. Roofing and exterior remodeling
3. Kitchen and bath remodeling
4. Flooring and tile installation
5. Glass and glazing
6. Plumbing / HVAC installation and service
7. Electrical contractors

## Market sizing: important caveat
The segments CSV contains broad U.S. employer-establishment counts from Census County Business Patterns 2023. These are physical locations, not unique companies. Some industries also show 2022 SUSB firm-count references from a secondary compilation. Do not add establishments and firms together.

The SBA Office of Advocacy reports 26.1% of employer firms were family-owned in 2022. That is a cross-industry benchmark, not a verified percentage for each trade.

No public source cleanly cross-tabulates family control, $5M+ revenue, 2–20 branches and owner-led operating style. Do not present a precise national target-account count as fact. Use an editable scenario model and validate named accounts individually.

## Prospect qualification
1. Is the company privately held? Who owns it?
2. How many actual branches/locations does it operate (not just service areas)?
3. Is annual revenue plausibly above $5M? What is the evidence and confidence?
4. Is leadership still close to daily operations?
5. Which workflows still rely on email, spreadsheets, phone calls or manual re-entry?
6. Which CRM, ERP, accounting and field-service systems are in place?
7. Is there an identifiable operational bottleneck and executive sponsor?

## Qualification discipline
- Mark ownership, revenue and branch count as unverified until supported.
- Keep public-company, PE roll-up and national franchise structures out of the core ICP unless there is a clear local decision-maker.
- All bundled prospect rows are fictional examples. Example.com links are placeholders.
