Why I suggest starting with estate readiness

Know who can help, decide, and benefit—before life changes.

I begin with estate questions because they connect so many parts of life: a home, children, aging parents, beneficiaries, and even a small business. Understanding who could act for you and where your wishes are documented is useful before considering any financial product. A living trust may fit some families but not others; a qualified California estate attorney handles legal advice and documents.
