Bring these to a conversation

Five questions worth asking.

Tap a question for a little context. These are educational prompts, not personalized recommendations.

What is guaranteed and what depends on assumptions that can change?

Ask for guaranteed figures separately from illustrated projections. Interest assumptions, charges and other non-guaranteed factors can change, so a hypothetical illustration is not a promise of future value.

How are value credits calculated, limited, and reduced by charges?

Review the specific crediting method, caps, participation rates, spreads and periods when value may not grow. Then compare those amounts with ongoing insurance and administrative charges.

What happens to coverage if premiums pause or underperforming years occur?

Ask what happens if planned premiums stop, credited returns are low, or charges rise. Request explanations of minimum funding, lapse risk, and any notice or options available under the contract.

How do loans and withdrawals affect the eventual benefit and taxes?

Policy loans and withdrawals may reduce cash value and the death benefit. They may also increase lapse risk or trigger taxes in some circumstances; request policy-specific written explanations from a properly licensed professional.

Is the complexity and ongoing cost appropriate for my original protection goal?

Return to the initial need for protection and compare costs, transparency, access restrictions and alternatives. A complex policy is not automatically the right choice simply because it can build cash value.

Keep in mind

No tax-free income, guaranteed growth, principal protection, or suitability claim is made here. Specific contract terms require a licensed professional review.

As life changes, revisit who can act for you and how your wishes will be carried out.
