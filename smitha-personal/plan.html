    Why these questions matter

    Good decisions start with understanding.

    Begin with your timeline, immediate responsibilities, existing financial safety net, and the decision you're trying to make. Those basics often reveal which questions deserve attention first.

    An example

    “I'm changing jobs. What should I think about first?”

    A job change can affect pay timing, health coverage, retirement benefits, and emergency savings. List the relevant deadlines and questions first, then compare options carefully.
