A little more clarity

Your five questions

Take these questions into a conversation with an appropriately licensed professional. They are not a personalized plan or a recommendation to buy any financial product.

    This tool is educational only. It does not calculate returns, make suitability assessments, recommend investments, or collect your financial information. Before working with any professional, verify licenses, registration status, fee arrangements and services.
    Why these questions matter

    Good decisions start with understanding.

    I believe the first conversation should help us understand what's changing, what matters most, and what still needs to be learned. There's no reason to start with a sales pitch.

    An example

    “I'm changing jobs. What should I think about first?”

    A thoughtful discussion may begin with goals, timing, existing benefits, responsibilities, risks, and questions to investigate—before discussing any product.
