Bring these to a conversation

Five questions worth asking.

Tap a question for a little context. These are educational prompts, not personalized recommendations.

What home costs should we plan for beyond the monthly payment?

Look beyond principal and interest: taxes, insurance, utilities, maintenance, assessments and emergency repairs can change. Compare these obligations with ongoing household cash flow.

How might we manage payments if a contributor could not work?

Consider emergency reserves, existing workplace benefits and any protection already in place. Discuss a realistic plan for the person who would handle the payment if a contributor became unavailable.

How is the property titled and what would happen to it later?

Ownership titles affect legal rights and what may happen on death or transfer. A California estate attorney can explain how your deed and other estate documents work together.

Does it belong in a living trust or another transfer arrangement?

Trust ownership or another transfer method may be appropriate in some cases, but changing a deed has legal and possible lending or tax implications. Have a qualified attorney review the options before retitling property.

Are current policies, beneficiaries, and documents coordinated?

Make sure property ownership, beneficiary designations, insurance arrangements and legal documents reflect current family intentions. Revisit them after a purchase, marriage, separation or other major event.

Keep in mind

Property transfer rules, trust funding, mortgages, and taxes require professional review for your circumstances.
