# Smitha Rangaswamy | Client-ready educational MVP
**Proposed package price:** $2,000 one-time initial MVP (business proposal, not an invoice or a signed agreement)

## Purpose
A warm, personal, self-guided education experience that helps Los Angeles residents explore estate readiness, trusts, protection, children, caregiving, retirement, Medicare Part B and business continuity, then contact Smitha when ready. This is not a carrier portal, a legal-service platform, or a software sales site.

## Included in this MVP
- Responsive 21-page personal website, original watercolor illustration, branded navigation and content.
- Estate preparation and living-trust decision questions as the most prominent starting point (trusts are not mandatory for everyone).
- Existing life-stage selector, personal question guide, topic search, checklists, official resources and interlinked articles.
- Six printable and shareable takeaways (estate, trusts, children, caregiving, retirement/Medicare, business).
- Eleven warm email templates in a browser-based draft studio with example personalization, copying, and compose-by-email actions.
- Direct call and text routes on every page, contextual prefilled message, optional STAR introduction, and quick mobile contact bar.
- Configurable email, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM, LinkedIn, X, TikTok and scheduler destinations, enabled **only** after the owner sets a verified live link.
- Fictional sample journey personas to demonstrate scenarios and acceptance tests, explicitly labeled as examples.
- GitHub source versioning, Render static deployment, release checklist and test-case matrix.

## What the $2,000 offer should NOT imply
The fee does not include a full CRM, automatic lead capture, server-side customer-data storage, identity verification, automatic WhatsApp/DM sending, real appointment confirmation, policy quoting, financial recommendations, or drafting/funding legal trusts. Live generative AI requires a separate API key, provider charges, and privacy review. Marketing claims, licenses, consent language, domain DNS and production legal/compliance approval must be signed off by Smitha and applicable experts.

## Owner configuration
Edit `site-config.js`. The phone listed on Smitha's business card is the voice contact. SMS capability must be confirmed. Social `exampleOnly` values are *fictional* and cannot be clicked. To activate a real social or scheduler channel, set its `url` to Smitha's verified HTTPS profile or booking link and `verified:true`.

The public contact interface shows only live verified channels; unverified placeholders remain hidden. If Smitha has a verified public email address, populate `contact.email.address` and `verified:true`.

## Content distribution
- `handouts.html`: select a guide, print/save as PDF, copy text, or share the URL.
- `outreach-kit.html`: select an email, personalize the example first name/context, edit copy, copy the draft or open your own email application.
- Templates are **public static content** in this demo. There is no private staff portal, contact list, or automatic mailing system. Use only with permission to contact recipients.
- Replace sample text with Smitha's reviewed and approved communications; do not add confidential client data to site files.

## Compliance and content ownership
- Confirm Smitha's license category, professional firm affiliation, permitted securities/insurance services, advertisements, fees and endorsements before public launch.
- California Courts: estate documents can include wills, powers of attorney, health directives, and trusts; the right combination depends on the situation. Qualified legal professionals should prepare/review trust documents. https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/wills-estates-probate/legal-documents
- California DOJ: trust creation is a personal decision, not mandatory. https://oag.ca.gov/consumers/general/estate-finance
- Medicare.gov: Part B enrollment rules and late penalties require official verification. https://www.medicare.gov/basics/costs/medicare-costs/avoid-penalties
- California DOI: long-term coverage and policy loans must be evaluated with costs, limits and risks. https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/105-type/95-guides/07-life/life-ins-guide.cfm
- FTC: material sponsorship and affiliate relationships require clear disclosure. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
